Our Story

Rooted in the historic lanes of Naya Bazaar, Ajmer, Ramchand Radhakishan has been a trusted name in premium dry fruits for generations.

What began as a humble family merchant shop built on honesty and warmth has grown into a cherished local landmark. For decades, we have welcomed generations of families seeking the purest, highest-quality nuts and dried fruits for their homes and celebrations.

Today, we bridge heritage with modern wellness. We hand-pick our harvest straight from the world's finest orchards—bringing you 100% organic, unpolished goodness. When you choose RR Dryfruits, you aren't just buying quality; you are taking home a piece of Ajmer’s enduring tradition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver outside Ajmer?

Yes! We offer secure, vacuum-sealed shipping across India to ensure your dry fruits arrive perfectly fresh.

How should I store my dry fruits?

For maximum freshness, transfer them to an airtight container and store them in a cool, dry place or refrigerate them.

Do you take bulk or wedding orders?

Absolutely. We specialize in custom gifting hampers and bulk orders for weddings, festivals, and corporate events.
